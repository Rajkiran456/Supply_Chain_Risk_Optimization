# 📦 Supply Chain & Inventory Risk Optimization

## 📌 Business Problem Statement
Inventory stockouts lead to revenue loss and customer dissatisfaction, while overstocking blocks working capital. The objective of this project is to calculate Reorder Points, identify high-risk SKUs facing imminent stockouts, and quantify the exact capital required for replenishment.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack & Tools
- **Language:** Python (Pandas, NumPy)
- **Concepts:** Inventory Management (Lead Time, Reorder Point), Risk Modeling, Data Analysis

## 📊 Key Insights & Business Impact
- **High-Risk SKUs Identified:** 4 out of 7 SKUs flagged for immediate replenishment.
- **Financial Capital Impact:** Quantified an immediate capital requirement of **₹2,69,400** to prevent stockout losses.
- **Operational Metric:** Reorder Points calculated dynamically based on daily demand and supplier lead times.
